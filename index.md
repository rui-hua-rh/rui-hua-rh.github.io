My name is Rui Hua. 

I obtained my Bacheler's degree in Instrumental Science at Beihang University (Beijing, China). During my senior year, I had ACL because of playing basketball. As an engineering student, I become enthusiastic about wearable technologies applied to health after the surgery. My research during my PhD study has been focusing on developing smart insoles and AI, applied to activity recognition, gait analysis, foot motion assessments, falling risk estimation, and medication in healthy seniors and patients with Parkinson's disease. I completed my PhD, advised by Dr. Ya Wang, at Nanorobotics, Energy Harvesting and Sensing (NES) Lab under J. Mike Walker ’66 Department of Mechanical Engineering at Texas A&M University. With lots of curiosity of how robust electronics were developed as products, I became an engineer working at MKS Instruments, which develops highly reliable and precise instrument for semiconductor manufacturing for over 60 years in the field. After almost two years in the industry learning through practice, I wanted to go back to research on wearable technologies. With lots of curiosity of how wearables used in clinical studies to help patients, I joined Shirley Ryan AbilityLab (SRALab) as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow, advised by Dr. Arun Jayaraman, PT, PhD, and Dr. Megan K. O'Brien, PhD at Max Näder Center for Rehabilitation Technologies and Outcomes Research.  

After exploring and learning from all my experiences, I want to be a professor. I enjoy working with students and helping them with my ability and experiences, expecially sharing any mistakes I made to help students aviod similar ones. 

I am currently a Postoctoral Research Fellow at SRALab and I am applying to faculty positions, with the goal of starting as an assistant professor in Fall 2025. 

My aim as an independent researcher is to develop smart wearable electronics, systems and predictive AI that deliver digital health and medicine solutions to improve the quality of life for aging and various patient populations through translational research.

My expertise: 
- Wearable electronics/systems design and prototype
- Biomedical signal processing and sensor data analysis
- Predictive AI algorithm development
- Human-in-the-loop real-time wearable computation

Please reach out if you are interested in my research and future collaborations.
